::part() and ::slotted()

Below is a custom element, <info-card>, built with an open shadow root. The component exposes named part attributes for the outer page to restyle, and uses ::slotted() internally to style whatever content is passed into its slots.

1. Both pseudo-elements working together

The blue header comes from info-card::part(header) in the page's stylesheet. The heading color and paragraph spacing come from ::slotted(h2) and ::slotted(p) inside the shadow tree.

2. Slot fallback content

This card passes in nothing at all. Every <slot> shows its fallback content instead.

3. The cascade gotcha

The shadow stylesheet colors ::slotted(p) gray, but this card's paragraph is bold red. That comes from an ordinary outer-page rule, info-card.outer-wins p. Slotted content lives in the light DOM, so the outer page wins any conflict with ::slotted().
